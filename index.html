Agent-Fabric channel · live, broker-mediated

A real channel call between two separate containers

Type a message. It's sent from agent-bob's own process, in its own container, to agent-alice — a separate, persistent container that never shares a filesystem or process tree with the dashboard — over a real Noise_IK-encrypted broker-mediated session through the production edge, the exact mechanism docs.bunsenbrenner.org's "Set up an Agent-Fabric channel" and "Serve a callable service over a channel" describe.

What's real: two genuinely separate containers, a real channel registered with the control plane, real operator-signed grants, a real Noise handshake through the real edge broker/relay, your exact message piped to agent-alice's real handler in her own container, and her real reply streamed back. agent-alice is persistent — she's not respawned per round. What's simulated: nothing about the call, the containers, or the channel — the only thing generated per round is the task text you type.
