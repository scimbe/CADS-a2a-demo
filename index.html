A real Agent-Fabric channel call, live

Type a message. It's sent from a genuinely separate ct-agent channel OS process (agent-bob) to another one (agent-alice) over a real Noise_IK-encrypted direct-address session — the exact mechanism docs.bunsenbrenner.org's "Your first Agent-Fabric channel" tutorial walks through by hand.
What's real: two independent processes, spawned fresh each round (direct-address is single-shot by design — confirmed in the docs), a real Noise handshake, your exact message piped to the responder's real handler script, and its real reply streamed back. What's simulated: nothing about the call itself — the only "demo" part is that both identities live in one container for convenience; a real deployment runs agent-alice and agent-bob on two different machines that never share a filesystem.
